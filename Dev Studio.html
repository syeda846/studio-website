How a project runs

Six stages, from your first message to ongoing support.

  1. Audit

    We learn your goals and review your current site or setup.

  2. Planning

    Scope, priorities and timeline agreed before work begins.

  3. UI/UX design

    Layouts and flows you can review before we build.

  4. Development

    Clean, tested code delivered step by step.

  5. Testing

    Quality checks across devices and real use cases.

  6. Deployment and support

    We launch it, then stay available for updates.
